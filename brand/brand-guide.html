01 — LOGO

The mark and the wordmark

Usage rules

White logo on dark surfaces (default) · black logo on light surfaces only
Full wordmark in headers and covers · DM mark for footers, favicons, avatars
Clearspace: height of the D on all sides · never recolour, stretch, or add effects
Nav size on web: 36px tall · footer mark: 28px tall

06 — SPACING, MOTION & VOICE

How it moves and speaks

Spacing & layout

Base grid: 4px — every measure is a multiple
Content width: max 1152px (max-w-6xl)
Page gutters: 24px mobile, 32px desktop
Section padding: 112–144px vertical
Card padding: 28px · grid gaps: 20px

Motion

Curve: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)
Hero entrance: rise 28px + fade, 0.9s, staggered 0.05–0.62s
Scroll reveal: rise 32px, 0.8s, at 15% visibility
Hovers: 0.25–0.35s · respects prefers-reduced-motion
Icons: Lucide, 1.5px stroke, monochrome only

Voice

Confident, intelligent, professional — not corporate, not casual
Lead with insight, then the offer
AI supports human decisions — never replaces them
No performance claims or guarantees
Short sentences. One idea per section.

Headline formula

Line one states the promise in bone white. Line two dims to mist. One word — the payoff — gets the teal gradient. Examples from the site: "Turn AI from experiment into your advantage" · "AI strategy that ships, not slideware" · "Get cited where your buyers now ask".
