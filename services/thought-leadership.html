THE PROBLEM

Buyers follow people.
Companies post into silence.

Look at your own feed. The content you stop for comes from individuals with a point of view — not from company pages announcing they're "thrilled to share". By the time a B2B buyer talks to sales, they've already formed an opinion of your company from what its people publish.

Every executive knows this. Almost none have the hours to do it well. So they post sporadically, sound corporate when they do, and watch less qualified voices own the conversations they should be leading.

The fix isn't more time. It's a system that extracts your thinking and publishes it consistently, in your voice.

HOW WE THINK

What separates authority
from noise

01

Point of view over platitudes

"Great insights, thanks for sharing" content builds nothing. Authority comes from saying what you actually think — including where you disagree with your industry's conventional wisdom.

02

Your voice is the moat

Feeds are flooding with generic AI-written posts, and readers can smell them. We use AI for research and leverage — but the voice, stories, and opinions are extracted from you, because that's what can't be copied.

03

Consistency beats virality

One viral post is a lottery ticket. Showing up three times a week for a year is a reputation. We optimise for the compound effect — being the name that comes to mind when your problem comes up.

04

Stories carry strategy

Frameworks get skimmed; stories get remembered. The most effective executive content wraps a lesson in something that actually happened — a deal, a mistake, a decision. Our interviews are built to surface those.

05

Distribution is a conversation

Posting is half the job. Thoughtful comments on the right people's posts put you in front of their audiences and start the relationships that become pipeline. That's why the engagement playbook ships with every retainer.
