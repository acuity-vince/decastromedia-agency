HOW WE THINK

The rules of ranking
inside the answer

01

Answer first, elaborate second

Answer engines lift passages, not pages. Content structured as direct answers — question, clear response, then supporting depth — is what gets extracted and cited. Burying the answer in paragraph six is invisibility.

02

Be an entity, not just a website

Models reason about entities: who you are, what you do, how you relate to your category. If your company's facts are inconsistent across the web, you're a blur — and blurs don't get recommended.

03

Original data earns citations

Engines cite sources that add something: proprietary research, real numbers, expert positions. Rewording what already ranks earns nothing. We build content assets that are the source, not a summary of one.

04

Third-party trust transfers

When answer engines assess who leads a category, they weigh what independent sources say. Placement in the publications and comparison resources models already trust moves you into answers faster than anything on your own domain.

05

Measure share of answer

Rankings and traffic don't capture AEO. The metric that matters: of the questions your buyers ask, in what share of answers do you appear — and how are you described when you do. That's what we track and report.
